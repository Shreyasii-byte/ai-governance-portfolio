# AI Governance Portfolio

A research-oriented portfolio exploring responsible AI development, governance frameworks, algorithmic fairness, risk management, and AI policy.

## About

This portfolio documents practical research and analytical projects examining how AI systems can be evaluated, governed, and deployed responsibly.

## Research Areas

- Algorithmic fairness and bias evaluation
- Generative AI risk and impact assessments
- AI risk management and accountability
- AI policy and regulatory analysis
- Responsible AI deployment

## Projects

| Project | Focus |
|---|---|
| 01. Recruitment AI Fairness Audit | Fairness, bias and accountability in AI hiring |
| 02. Generative AI Impact Assessment | Risks of AI-powered customer support |
| 03. AI Risk Management Case Study | Applying the NIST AI RMF |
| 04. AI Policy Analysis | Evaluating AI governance policies |

## Frameworks and References

- NIST AI Risk Management Framework
- EU AI Act
- OECD AI Principles
- Responsible AI and algorithmic accountability research

## Purpose

To develop practical research and analytical capabilities in AI governance, policy evaluation, risk assessment, and responsible AI.
