# AI Governance Portfolio

**Independent Research | AI Policy | Responsible AI | Risk Management**

A research portfolio exploring how artificial intelligence systems can be evaluated, governed and deployed responsibly.

My work focuses on algorithmic fairness, AI risk assessment, governance frameworks, policy research and organisational accountability.
