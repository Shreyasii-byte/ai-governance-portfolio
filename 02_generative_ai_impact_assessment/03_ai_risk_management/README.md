# Project 03: Applying the NIST AI Risk Management Framework

## Overview

This project applies the NIST AI Risk Management Framework (AI RMF) to a hypothetical AI-powered loan approval system.

The case study examines how financial organisations can identify, assess, manage, and monitor risks associated with AI-assisted lending decisions.

## Research Question

How can an organisation apply the NIST AI RMF to identify and manage risks in an AI-powered loan approval system?

## Objectives

- Apply the four core functions of the NIST AI RMF.
- Identify risks associated with automated lending decisions.
- Evaluate potential impacts on applicants and financial institutions.
- Recommend governance controls and risk mitigation measures.
- Develop an organisational AI governance action plan.

## Framework

The assessment uses four NIST AI RMF functions:

1. GOVERN — Establish accountability, policies and oversight.
2. MAP — Understand the AI system, context and potential impacts.
3. MEASURE — Evaluate and analyse identified risks.
4. MANAGE — Prioritise risks and implement mitigation measures.

## Case Study

**System:** AI-powered loan approval system.

**Organisation:** Hypothetical financial institution.

**Purpose:** Support loan eligibility assessment and application processing.

## Deliverables

- NIST AI RMF case study
- AI risk assessment matrix
- Governance action plan
- Deployment recommendations

## Limitations

This is a hypothetical educational case study. It does not evaluate a real financial institution or establish regulatory compliance.

## Conclusion

The project demonstrates how the NIST AI RMF can be translated into practical organisational governance activities for high-impact AI systems.
