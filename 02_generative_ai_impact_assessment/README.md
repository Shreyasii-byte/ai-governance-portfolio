# Project 02: Generative AI Impact Assessment

## Overview

This project evaluates the governance, ethical, operational, and societal risks associated with deploying a Generative AI-powered customer support chatbot.

The hypothetical system assists customers with product queries, refunds, complaints, and account-related questions.

The assessment examines whether the system can be deployed responsibly, what safeguards are required, and when human intervention should be mandatory.

## Research Question

What risks can arise when organisations deploy Generative AI for customer support, and what governance controls are necessary to ensure responsible use?

## Objectives

- Identify risks associated with Generative AI deployment.
- Assess potential harm to customers and organisations.
- Evaluate privacy, security, fairness, and reliability concerns.
- Develop a structured AI risk register.
- Recommend human oversight and escalation mechanisms.
- Make an evidence-based deployment recommendation.

## Scope

**System:** AI-powered customer support chatbot.

**Intended users:** Customers and customer support teams.

**Key functions:**
- Answering product-related questions.
- Explaining refund and return policies.
- Handling customer complaints.
- Retrieving account-related information.
- Escalating complex cases to human agents.

## Assessment Areas

1. Intended use and system boundaries
2. Stakeholder identification
3. Risk identification
4. Risk likelihood and severity
5. Mitigation and safeguards
6. Human oversight
7. Accountability and monitoring
8. Deployment recommendation

## Governance References

- NIST AI Risk Management Framework
- OECD AI Principles
- EU AI Act

These frameworks are used as analytical references, not as a claim of legal compliance.

## Expected Deliverables

- AI Impact Assessment
- AI Risk Register
- Mitigation and Human Oversight Plan
- Deployment Recommendation

## Limitations

This is a hypothetical educational assessment. No real company system, customer data, or production chatbot has been evaluated.

## Conclusion

The project demonstrates how organisations can assess risks before deploying Generative AI systems and establish appropriate governance safeguards.
