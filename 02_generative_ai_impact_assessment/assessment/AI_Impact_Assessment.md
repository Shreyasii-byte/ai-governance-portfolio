# AI Impact Assessment

## 1. System Description

**System name:** Customer Support AI Assistant

**System type:** Generative AI chatbot

**Purpose:** To automate routine customer support interactions and assist human support agents.

**Inputs:** Customer questions, product information, support documentation, and account-related information where authorised.

**Outputs:** Generated responses, recommendations, and escalation suggestions.

## 2. Intended Use

The chatbot is intended to:
- Answer frequently asked questions.
- Explain company policies.
- Provide basic troubleshooting guidance.
- Help customers understand refund procedures.
- Route complex complaints to human agents.

## 3. Out-of-Scope Uses

The chatbot must not independently:
- Make final decisions on disputed refunds.
- Change sensitive account information without authorisation.
- Provide guaranteed legal or financial advice.
- Deny customer complaints without human review.

## 4. Stakeholder Analysis

| Stakeholder | Potential Benefit | Potential Harm |
|---|---|---|
| Customers | Faster support | Incorrect information |
| Support agents | Reduced workload | Over-reliance on AI |
| Organisation | Lower support costs | Reputational damage |
| Vulnerable users | 24/7 access | Difficulty challenging AI responses |

## 5. Impact Assessment

### Privacy
Customer conversations may contain personal or sensitive information.

Potential impact: Unauthorised access, excessive data collection, or data exposure.

### Reliability
The chatbot may generate incorrect or fabricated information.

Potential impact: Customers may act on misleading refund or product guidance.

### Fairness and Accessibility
The chatbot may respond differently depending on language, communication style, or accessibility needs.

Potential impact: Unequal customer experiences.

### Accountability
Customers may not know whether an answer came from AI or who is responsible for errors.

Potential impact: Difficulty challenging decisions or obtaining remedies.

## 6. Human Oversight

- Provide an accessible human-agent escalation option.
- Require human review for disputed or high-impact decisions.
- Allow customers to challenge incorrect responses.
- Maintain clear responsibility for chatbot outputs.

## 7. Monitoring Plan

Monitor:
- Incorrect response frequency.
- Customer complaint trends.
- Human escalation rates.
- Privacy and security incidents.
- Performance across languages and user groups.

## 8. Preliminary Deployment Recommendation

**Conditional Go**

Deployment should proceed only after appropriate safeguards are implemented and tested.

This is a preliminary recommendation based on the hypothetical system design, not a validated production assessment.
