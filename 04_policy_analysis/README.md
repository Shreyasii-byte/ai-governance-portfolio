# Project 04: Comparative AI Policy Analysis

## Overview

This project compares two approaches to AI governance:

- United Kingdom: A context-driven, pro-innovation approach to AI regulation.
- European Union: A harmonised, risk-based legislative approach through the EU AI Act.

The purpose is to examine how different governance approaches address innovation, safety, accountability, transparency and regulatory oversight.

## Research Question

How do the United Kingdom and European Union approaches to AI governance differ, and what are the implications for innovation, accountability and responsible AI deployment?

## Objectives

- Examine the policy objectives of both jurisdictions.
- Compare their approaches to AI risk management.
- Analyse accountability and institutional oversight.
- Evaluate implications for organisations developing AI systems.
- Identify strengths, limitations and policy trade-offs.
- Develop evidence-based policy recommendations.

## Primary Policy Sources

1. UK Government — A Pro-Innovation Approach to AI Regulation (2023).
2. European Union — Regulation (EU) 2024/1689, the Artificial Intelligence Act.

Use the official published documents as primary evidence.

## Research Dimensions

- Regulatory philosophy
- Risk classification
- Accountability
- Transparency
- Human oversight
- Innovation
- Enforcement
- Implementation challenges

## Deliverables

- Comparative policy analysis
- Policy comparison matrix
- Policy recommendations memo

## Limitations

This is an independent educational policy analysis, not legal advice. Conclusions must be checked against the original policy documents and subsequent amendments or implementation guidance.

## Research Purpose

To develop skills in policy research, comparative analysis, evidence evaluation and AI governance recommendations.
