# Project 05: AI Governance Strategy and Policy Brief

## Overview

This project brings together insights from four AI governance research projects to develop an organisational AI governance strategy.

It focuses on translating AI governance principles into practical policies, responsibilities, risk controls and oversight mechanisms.

## Objectives

- Identify key AI governance risks across sectors.
- Map stakeholders and accountability.
- Develop an organisational AI governance structure.
- Recommend risk management and oversight mechanisms.
- Communicate recommendations through an executive policy brief.

## Research Areas

- Algorithmic fairness
- Generative AI risks
- AI risk management
- AI regulation and policy
- Organisational accountability

## Deliverables

1. AI Governance Map
2. Executive Policy Brief
3. Governance recommendations

## Conclusion

The project demonstrates how AI governance research can inform practical organisational decision-making and responsible AI deployment.

**Project type:** Independent AI Governance Research
