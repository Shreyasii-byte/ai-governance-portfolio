# AI Governance Map: Five Risks Organisations Should Assess Before Deployment

## 1. Algorithmic Bias

**Risk:** AI systems may produce discriminatory outcomes because of biased training data or design choices.

**Potential impact:** Unequal access to employment, financial services or other opportunities.

**Governance controls:**
- Evaluate outcomes across relevant demographic groups.
- Document data limitations.
- Conduct periodic fairness audits.
- Provide mechanisms to challenge consequential decisions.

**Responsible stakeholders:** AI developers, HR teams, compliance teams and senior management.

## 2. Privacy and Data Protection

**Risk:** AI systems may collect, process or expose personal information inappropriately.

**Potential impact:** Loss of privacy, unauthorised disclosure and reduced trust.

**Governance controls:**
- Data minimisation.
- Access controls.
- Privacy impact assessment.
- Secure data handling.

**Responsible stakeholders:** Data protection officers, security teams and system owners.

## 3. Reliability and Hallucinations

**Risk:** Generative AI systems may produce inaccurate or fabricated information.

**Potential impact:** Misleading customer advice and harmful decisions.

**Governance controls:**
- Test outputs against reliable sources.
- Establish human review for high-impact outputs.
- Monitor error rates.
- Provide clear escalation channels.

**Responsible stakeholders:** Product owners, AI teams and operational managers.

## 4. Lack of Accountability

**Risk:** Organisations may fail to clearly assign responsibility for AI-related decisions and harms.

**Potential impact:** Individuals may struggle to challenge decisions or obtain remedies.

**Governance controls:**
- Define system ownership.
- Maintain decision documentation.
- Establish complaint and escalation procedures.
- Assign incident response responsibilities.

**Responsible stakeholders:** Senior management, legal teams and compliance officers.

## 5. Inadequate Human Oversight

**Risk:** People may over-rely on AI outputs without meaningful review.

**Potential impact:** Errors may become consequential decisions without appropriate intervention.

**Governance controls:**
- Define human review requirements.
- Train relevant employees.
- Enable intervention and escalation.
- Monitor human-AI decision processes.

**Responsible stakeholders:** Operational managers, system owners and governance committees.

## Cross-Cutting Recommendation

Organisations should assess AI risks before deployment and throughout the system lifecycle.

Governance should combine technical testing, organisational accountability, human oversight and continuous monitoring.
