# AI Governance Action Plan

## Objective

Establish an organisational governance process for the responsible deployment of an AI-powered loan approval system.

| Priority | Action | Responsible Team | Expected Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| High | Establish AI governance committee | Senior Management | Governance charter |
| High | Conduct fairness evaluation | AI Risk Team | Fairness assessment |
| High | Implement human review | Credit Operations | Review procedure |
| High | Strengthen applicant data protection | Security Team | Access control policy |
| Medium | Document model limitations | Model Development Team | Model documentation |
| Medium | Establish ongoing monitoring | AI Risk Team | Monitoring reports |
| Medium | Define incident escalation process | Compliance Team | Incident response procedure |

## Governance Success Criteria

- Every major AI risk has an assigned owner.
- High-priority risks have documented mitigation measures.
- Human review procedures are operational.
- Model performance and fairness are periodically evaluated.
- Incidents can be reported and escalated.
- Deployment decisions are documented.

## Final Recommendation

The system should not be deployed until critical risks have been assessed and appropriate safeguards are operational.

This action plan is an educational proposal, not a formal compliance determination.
