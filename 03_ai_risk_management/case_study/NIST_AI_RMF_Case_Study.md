# NIST AI RMF Case Study: AI-Powered Loan Approval

## 1. Scenario

A hypothetical financial institution introduces an AI system to support loan application assessments.

The system analyses applicant information and generates loan eligibility recommendations for human credit officers.

The organisation wants to improve processing efficiency while maintaining fairness, transparency, privacy and accountability.

## 2. GOVERN — Establish Accountability

**Key question:** Who is responsible for AI-related risks?

Recommended actions:

- Establish an AI governance committee.
- Assign responsibility to model owners and risk officers.
- Define policies for responsible AI use.
- Establish human review procedures.
- Maintain documentation of model decisions and changes.
- Define escalation and incident reporting processes.

**Expected outcome:** Clear ownership and accountability for AI risks.

## 3. MAP — Understand the Context

**Key question:** What does the system do, and who could be affected?

| Area | Assessment |
|---|---|
| Intended purpose | Support loan eligibility assessment |
| Users | Credit officers |
| Affected stakeholders | Loan applicants |
| Data | Applicant financial and application information |
| Potential impact | Access to financial services |
| Key concerns | Bias, privacy, explainability and errors |

The organisation should document the system's intended use, limitations, stakeholders and operating environment.

**Expected outcome:** A documented understanding of the system and its potential impacts.

## 4. MEASURE — Evaluate Risks

**Key question:** How can the organisation evaluate AI risks?

Recommended evaluations:

- Examine data quality and representativeness.
- Test outcomes across relevant applicant groups.
- Evaluate false approvals and false rejections.
- Assess model explainability.
- Review privacy and security controls.
- Monitor performance and reliability.

Metrics should be selected according to the system's intended purpose and identified risks.

**Expected outcome:** Evidence-based understanding of system risks.

## 5. MANAGE — Respond to Risks

**Key question:** What should the organisation do about identified risks?

Recommended actions:

- Prioritise high-severity risks.
- Introduce human review for consequential decisions.
- Establish procedures for correcting errors.
- Restrict access to sensitive applicant data.
- Monitor model performance after deployment.
- Define procedures to suspend or withdraw the system if necessary.

**Expected outcome:** Risks are prioritised, addressed and continuously monitored.

## 6. Deployment Recommendation

**Recommendation: Conditional Go**

The organisation should consider deployment only after establishing appropriate governance, validating system performance, evaluating fairness, and implementing meaningful human oversight.

Automated recommendations should not become unchallengeable decisions.

## 7. Limitations

- The organisation and system are hypothetical.
- No real loan applicant dataset was evaluated.
- Risk ratings are illustrative.
- The assessment does not establish legal or regulatory compliance.

## 8. Conclusion

The NIST AI RMF provides a structured approach to managing AI risks throughout the system lifecycle.

Applying GOVERN, MAP, MEASURE and MANAGE helps organisations move beyond technical model performance and consider accountability, stakeholder impact and responsible deployment.
